I feel like sonic frontiers on release had quite a bit of issues with it's physics and this game made me realize how important momentum is in a Sonic game 

This mod makes it so that sonic frontiers physics feel more polished and less janky.  I've made changes not only to the open zone but cyberspace and to even the combat as well.  This is my first mod that I have ever created so sorry if it isn't that great.

Changes:

Open Zone:
-Slope Physics has been integrated this mod allowing sonic to gain momentum down slopes and loses momentum up slopes.

-The speed of the boosting animation and step rate has been increased to make it feel like when sonic is boosting he is going faster than his running state.

-Boost/Boost Max and Spin-dash cap has been increased so you can reach higher top speeds. You might realise you start of relatively slow with the boost but as you start to use it longer you gain even more speed than you usually could.

-Drop Dash rotation has been increased and it feels a lot better than it did than in the vanilla game.

-Jump braking has been heavily reduced allowing your to gain even more keep even more speed while jumping especially while boosting at full speed.

-Light speed dash has been improved so it doesn't brake like it usually does.

-Homing attack speed has been increased making the gameplay feel more dynamic and more like a 3D sonic game.

-Boost gauge dissipation has been increased to allow for more challenge and difficulty making you have to use your momentum as an alternative to your boost in certain scenarios this adds more variety in sonic move-set instead of just constantly boosting to reach high speeds this personally for me was a major issue for the boost games and why most of them didn't feel as satisfying as the adventure games.

-Jump rotation has also been fixed to feel tighter and more flexible leading to a more fun play-style while also making platforming a whole lot easier for people who were struggling with it.

-Air boost has been changed to hold out for a longer and (open zone only)

-Wall walking and running/boosting has been made significantly faster to speed up the game and feel more canon to how fast sonic can actually climb up structures since I feel like running up structures in vanilla frontiers without boosting was too slow compared to with this mod.

-Momentum has been added to the grind rails so the longer you stay on them the faster you can go  allowing you to reach even higher speeds than your could have in the original game without having to always rely on having the use the boost to go fast.(there is a limit however)


Cyberspace:

-Changed the physics to feel more faster and feel like a sonic game combining different aspects from sonic games into this one mod.

-Drifting has been made so that it doesn't completely slow you down like the vanilla game did for some strange reason.

-Boarding has been made faster for all the sa2 fans out there who really hated how the boarding felt like in the vanilla game.

-I made air boost last a *very* short amount of time but send you a decent distance like in sonic gens/unleashed.
